A clear note about your information

Your privacy
matters here.

This page describes what happens on Find Your Niche today, and what will need to be explained if optional analytics or advertising tools are enabled later.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

What this site does today

The self-discovery quiz, profile matching, and Exam Explorer selections run in your browser. Your answers and choices are used to filter the example data and show results on the page; this static site does not send them to a Find Your Niche account or save them in a database. The example stories and exam list are read from JSON files hosted with this site.

The Resources page contains visual spaces reserved for future ads. Those spaces are placeholders only: no advertising tags or ad network scripts are active at this time. The optional Google Analytics code is also inactive until a site-specific measurement ID is entered and the production bundle is rebuilt. There are no active affiliate links on the tools page.

Website hosting and fonts

The provider that hosts this website may process routine technical information, such as an IP address, browser details, and the time and page requested, to deliver and protect the site. Hosting and log retention practices depend on the provider used to publish the site.

This site requests its typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser loads those font files, it connects to Google and may share ordinary request information with that service. See Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s handling of data.

If analytics is enabled

If a Google Analytics 4 measurement ID is added, Google Analytics will receive website usage information through its tag. Depending on the configuration, this can include page and session activity, device and browser details, approximate location, and identifiers such as the first-party _ga cookie. The current placeholder sends no analytics data.

Before enabling analytics, this notice should be updated to describe the live configuration, retention settings, and any choices or consent mechanisms that apply. See Google’s Analytics data collection overview.

If advertising is enabled

If ads are added later, advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to serve and measure ads. Google and other ad partners may use cookies to personalize ads based on visits to this or other sites, depending on the settings and choices in effect. No such ad tags are active on this site today.

Before ads go live, this page should identify the active vendors and explain the relevant data use and user choices. Visitors can manage Google personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Google’s publisher guidance explains its privacy policy disclosures for ads and publisher privacy disclosures. Consent requirements vary by location and setup; any required consent choices should be in place before relevant tags are used.

External tools and possible referral links

The tools page links to services operated by other organizations. When you follow an external link, that organization’s own privacy policy and terms apply. The affiliate URL spaces on that page are inactive placeholders. If a future referral link earns a commission, the relationship will be disclosed beside it; a provider may also use its own cookies or tracking when you visit its site.

Questions

For a question about this privacy notice, contact the site owner, Harshit Shrivastava, through the channel where you found Find Your Niche.
