A note from the founder

Why this
exists.

I made Find Your Niche because I know how heavy it can feel to be asked what you’re doing with your life when you’re still trying to work it out yourself.

As a student, I wanted something that felt more like a kind conversation than another list of rules. A place to get curious, hear that other people feel this way too, and take one small step without needing to know the whole route.

If you’re here feeling lost, I hope this reminds you that you’re allowed to begin from exactly where you are.

You’ve got time. — Harshit Shrivastava
